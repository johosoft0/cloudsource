Terms of Service

Last updated: May 2026

Who can use CloudSource

You must be at least 13 years old to use CloudSource. By using the app you agree to these terms.

Your reports

Reports must reflect actual weather conditions at your location. Submitting false, misleading, or offensive content may result in account removal. Photos must be of weather conditions — offensive or inappropriate images will be flagged by the community and removed.

Content you submit

You retain ownership of your photos and content. By submitting a report, you grant CloudSource a license to display it to other users and to include the structured observation data (condition, intensity, location, time) in anonymized, aggregated datasets.

Account termination

We may suspend or remove accounts that abuse the reporting system, spam votes, upload offensive content, or otherwise degrade the experience for other users.

No warranty

CloudSource is provided as-is. Weather reports come from other users and may not be accurate. Do not rely on CloudSource for safety-critical weather decisions.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Email address — used only for sign-in via magic link. We do not send marketing emails.

Location — your GPS coordinates are fuzzed by approximately 100 meters before being stored with a report. We never store your precise location. Cached location data on your device is also fuzzed.

Photos — attached to weather reports, stored for up to 48 hours, then automatically deleted from our servers.

Usage data — report submissions, votes, XP, and achievement progress are stored to power the app's features.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name (display names are chosen by you), contacts, browsing history, device identifiers, or any data beyond what's listed above.

How we use data

Your reports are shown to other users on the map. Anonymized, aggregated weather observation data (condition, intensity, time, approximate location, confidence scores) may be used to improve weather services and shared with third parties such as weather forecasters, researchers, and data services. Individual user information is never sold or shared.

Data retention

Active reports are visible for up to 6 hours. After 24 hours, reports are archived. Photos are deleted after 48 hours. Archived observation data (without photos or user identifiers) is retained indefinitely for weather analysis.

Your rights

You can delete your own reports at any time from within the app. To delete your account entirely, contact us and we will remove your profile and all associated data.

Contact

CloudSource is built and operated by Joe Collins. For questions about these terms or your data, reach out via the app or at the contact information on our website.
